The Reality

This Process Rewards Strategy — Not Just Grades

Most applicants treat law school applications like a numbers game: submit your LSAT and GPA and hope for the best. That's how people get rejected from schools they should have gotten into.

The applicants who win — who get into top-10 schools and negotiate six-figure scholarship packages — are the ones who understand that admissions committees are reading for a person, not a profile. They craft a cohesive narrative. They know which addenda to write and which to skip. They approach scholarship negotiation as a second application.

That's exactly what we teach — and do — for our clients.
