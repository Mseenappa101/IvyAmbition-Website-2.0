The Reality

The Transfer Path Starts Before You Start Law School

Most 1Ls who want to transfer don't start thinking about it until spring semester — by which point they've already made decisions that hurt their chances. Study group composition, course selection, professor relationships, exam strategy: all of it matters from day one.

IvyAmbition's transfer program is the only offering built around this reality. We work with you from the moment you start 1L — helping you maximize your GPA, position yourself for strong LORs, and execute a transfer application that tells the right story.

We've been through Columbia Law. We know what the process looks like from both sides.
