The Admissions Process Is Designed to Overwhelm You

You have one shot per cycle at each school. One personal statement to capture who you are in 2 pages. One school list that determines your options for the next 3 years and the trajectory of your entire career. One set of supplementals, each with a different prompt and a different word limit and a different unstated expectation.

And you're supposed to figure all of this out while working, studying for the LSAT, managing recommendations, hitting deadlines, and pretending you're not stressed.

The applicants who get into T-14 schools aren't the ones who figured it out alone. They're the ones who had someone in their corner who had done it before and could tell them exactly what to do, when to do it, and what not to waste time on.

That's what this is.
