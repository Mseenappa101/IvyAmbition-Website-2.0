Transfer Admissions Is the Highest-Stakes, Shortest-Window Process in Law School

You have one shot. The transfer window opens and closes in weeks — not months. Your 1L grades just came in and suddenly you're facing the biggest decision of your law school career with almost no time to execute.

And the process is nothing like initial admissions. Different essays. Different evaluation criteria. Different financial aid landscape. Different timeline. Every piece of advice you got as a 0L is either irrelevant or actively harmful when applied to transfers.

The 1Ls who successfully transfer into T-14 schools don't figure it out alone. They have someone who's done it before, who knows what transfer committees actually look for, and who can execute alongside them at the speed the transfer window demands.

That's what the Elite Transfer Program is.

FAQ

Common Questions

"How many clients do you take per transfer cycle?"
Very few. The transfer window is short and each client needs intensive, daily-level attention. Once we're full, we close enrollment immediately. This isn't a marketing tactic — it's a genuine constraint.
"What if my grades aren't strong enough to transfer?"
We'll tell you on the strategy call. We have no interest in taking your money if the outcomes aren't realistic. We'd rather direct you to the right level of support — a single strategy session, a workshop, or just the free tools — than overpromise.
"Is $5,000 worth it?"
The average difference in starting salary between a T-50 school and a T-14 school is roughly $80,000–$100,000 per year. Over a 10-year career, that's potentially $500,000–$1,000,000 in additional earnings. If this program helps you make that leap, the ROI on $5,000 isn't even close. And compared to the $8,000–$15,000 the big firms charge for less specialized service, this is the best value in transfer admissions.
"What if I get waitlisted?"
Waitlist strategy is included in the program. Transfer waitlists move fast — often in late July and August with almost no notice. We execute immediately with a Letter of Continued Interest, additional materials, and a communication plan.
"Can I just buy individual services instead?"
Absolutely. We offer everything from $150 resume reviews to $750 full application reviews. But the math favors the full program for anyone applying to multiple schools — and the unlimited messaging and AI platform aren't available à la carte. Check our transfer services page to see all options.
"How is this different from the Full Transfer Application Review ($750)?"
The $750 review is a one-time audit of your completed application. The Elite Transfer Program is an end-to-end partnership from strategy through submission and post-decision — with a dedicated coach, unlimited messaging, and AI tools. It's the difference between getting feedback and having a team.
